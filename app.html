About this

Each discount applies to the already reduced price, so the remaining fractions multiply: final = price x (1 - d1) x (1 - d2) ... Checked against published examples, such as successive discounts of 10% and 20% equal one 28% discount (example). Order does not change the result. Tax and fees are not included; some stores take coupons off the original price instead, so check how your coupon applies. Runs in your browser.
